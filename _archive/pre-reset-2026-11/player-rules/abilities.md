# Abilities

A character's ability is the single trait that sets them apart from an ordinary person of their class and clan. It governs:

- **Non-combat actions** (Section 10) — a character attempting an action tied to their ability rolls at standard odds; a character without the ability can still try, but at a steep penalty, and some actions may simply be closed to them at the referee's discretion.
- **Flavor and roleplay** — how your character is exceptional, independent of class. A Strength Caster and an Intelligence Caster read very differently at the table even though their spell list is identical.

**The six abilities, in full:**

- **Strength** — physical power. Bend iron bars, lift a portcullis, force open a stuck door.
- **Constitution** — health and endurance. Holding breath, swimming rough water, shrugging off adversity; a character with this ability gets +1 on saving dice against disease or poison.
- **Dexterity** — speed, balance, coordination. Climbing, sneaking, sleight of hand.
- **Wisdom** — intuition and perception. Spotting clues, noticing an ambush, telling when someone's lying.
- **Charisma** — force of personality. Leading, persuading, striking deals.
- **Intelligence** — knowledge and recall. Knowing the name of an artifact on sight, recalling a relevant legend, recognizing a magical effect from lore rather than trial and error.
