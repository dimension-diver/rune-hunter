# Advancement

Characters don't advance by counting kills or gold. A character reaches a new level by completing a milestone appropriate to their class and story — earning a guild rank, training an apprentice, passing a trial suited to what they do. There's no other path to a level: a player who never pursues the milestone simply never reaches that level. Nothing else needs to enforce that; it falls out naturally from there being only one road in.

What a level actually grants — HP, MP, combat moves, spells, crafting access — is exactly what's listed under each class in Section 4. Advancement only changes *how* you get there, never what's waiting at the top.

A milestone's completion is always narrated as part of a downtime beat (Section 12), not a separate transaction bolted onto one — the guild ceremony, the lesson from a mentor, the training montage between adventures. Leveling up happens in town, in the story, not the instant the last die lands in a dungeon.
