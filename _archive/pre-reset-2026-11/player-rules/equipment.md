# Equipment & Treasure

**Universal starting kit** (every character, regardless of class): rations, torches, a coin purse (Tier I) to spend on incidentals, keep, or bank in town. Rope, a bedroll, and similar extras are optional — bundle up to three small items per slot if you want to carry them.

Each class below lists a ready-made starting loadout. Take it as written, or swap items of equal slot cost within your class's weapon/armor rules.

**Hunter** — any armor, any weapon
- Chainmail, plus a choice of: shield + one-handed melee weapon of your choice, **or** a two-handed melee weapon of your choice

**Delver** — leather only, any one-handed weapon
- Leather armor, any two one-handed weapons of your choice (melee or ranged — a shortsword and pistol, dual pistols, dual daggers, whatever fits the character), lockpicks

**Caster** — no armor, dagger/staff or any one-handed weapon
- Staff, spellbook

**Seeker** — leather only, any one-handed weapon
- Leather armor, any two one-handed weapons of your choice, lockpicks

**Shaman** — no armor, staff only
- Staff

**Ranger** — no heavy armor/shields, no two-handed melee (ranged two-handers fine)
- Rifle or carbine, leather armor, a one-handed backup weapon of your choice — kept in a body slot for instant access, or in the pack instead (costs a round to retrieve)

**Striker** — no armor, bare hands or quarterstaff
- Bare-handed by default (needed to deliver spell-on-hit), or a quarterstaff if you'd rather have the extra damage
