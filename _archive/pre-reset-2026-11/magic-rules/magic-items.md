# Magic Items

**Scrolls.** Reading one performs the spell as if cast by a 9th-level Caster, and the writing fades after a single use. Anyone literate can read a scroll, but only an NPC specialist can write one.

**Potions.** Single-use, no MP cost, functions as the spell it's named for cast at 9th level unless its own description says otherwise.

**Staves.** Carry a limited number of charges (25 is the default unless a specific staff says otherwise). Recharging a spent staff costs 10% of its total value, whether the owner does it themselves (by casting spells directly into it) or pays an NPC caster to do it.

**Wands.** Function the same way as staves unless a specific wand's description says otherwise.

**Rings.** No universal mechanic established yet — each ring's effect is unique and self-contained; treat case-by-case until a general rule proves necessary.

**Mixed magic.** A catch-all category for items that don't fit the above — each is its own unique effect, usable by whichever classes its description allows.
