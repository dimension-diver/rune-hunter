# Spell List

This is the full spell list — Caster picks up a new one every level, and there's enough here to draw from for a long campaign. Duration is "1 turn" (10 rounds) unless noted; most durations and effects scale with level as described.

### Seeker, Shaman, Striker

All three draw from the Caster list above rather than having their own spell list. What differs is acquisition rate and MP pool, per Section 4: Shaman gains a new spell every level like Caster; Seeker and Striker gain new spells at 3rd, 6th, and 9th level instead. Seeker and Striker are also restricted to spells they can plausibly use within their own casting method (Striker's spells all resolve as self/touch effects, delivered through a bare-handed strike).
